Qué comprueba la verificación de lote

Every vial that leaves Codex Research carries a batch number, and every batch has its own certificate of analysis. The lookup above confirms three things: that the batch number exists in our records, which compound and strength it corresponds to, and which analytical tests were run on it.

It is a traceability check, not a test result in itself. The underlying document is the certificate of analysis, which we send in full on request before you pay.

Dónde encontrar tu número de lote

El número de lote va impreso en la etiqueta del vial, debajo del nombre del compuesto, con el formato CDX-NNNN, por ejemplo CDX-1017. Ese mismo número aparece en el certificado de análisis de ese lote. Si los dos no coinciden, el certificado no corresponde al vial que tienes en la mano.

Certificado del lote frente a certificado genérico

A certificate of analysis is only meaningful when it is tied to the batch in your hand. A generic certificate shows what the compound looked like on some production run, which may not be the one you received. A batch-specific certificate reports the tests run on that particular lot, and its batch number matches your label.

This is the difference worth checking with any supplier: ask whether the certificate is for your batch number, and whether the testing laboratory is independent of the seller.

En qué consisten los análisis

Dos métodos analíticos sostienen casi todo el peso de un certificado de péptidos:

  • High-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) separates the sample into its components and measures how much of the total corresponds to the target peptide. This is where a purity figure such as 99% comes from. Qué significa 99% por HPLC.
  • Mass spectrometry measures the molecular mass of what was in the vial and compares it against the expected mass of the compound. HPLC says how pure, mass spectrometry says whether it is the right molecule. Cómo se usa la espectrometría de masas.

A certificate that reports purity without confirming identity leaves the more important question open. How to read a certificate of analysis, line by line.

Si tu número de lote no aparece

Check for typos first: the format is three letters, a four-digit year and month, and a three-digit sequence. If it still does not resolve, send us a photograph of the label on WhatsApp and we will trace it. A number that does not exist in our records is not a Codex Research batch.

Preguntas sobre la verificación de lote

¿El certificado de análisis es específico de mi lote?

Yes. Each batch is tested separately and has its own certificate, identified by the same batch number printed on the vial label.

¿Quién hace los análisis?

An independent third-party laboratory, using HPLC and mass spectrometry. We do not test our own material and report on it.

¿Puedo ver el certificado antes de pagar?

Yes. Ask for it on WhatsApp with your batch number, or with the compound name if you have not ordered yet, and we send the full document.

¿Qué significa 99% de pureza por HPLC?

It means that in the chromatographic separation, 99% of the area measured corresponds to the target peptide. It is a measure of how much of the sample is the intended compound relative to other species detected by the method.

¿El número de lote demuestra que el vial es auténtico?

It proves the number belongs to a real batch of ours and tells you what that batch was. Combined with the certificate for that same number, it is the strongest check available short of testing the material yourself.
